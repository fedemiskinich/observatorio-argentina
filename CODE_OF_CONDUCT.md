# Code of Conduct

Observatorio Argentina welcomes technical, academic and professional collaboration in a respectful environment.

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, document claims and data provenance, respect privacy and applicable law, and avoid harassment, discrimination, doxxing, unauthorized access, or contributions intended to facilitate targeting of private individuals.

Project maintainers may reject or remove contributions that conflict with these principles, third-party terms, security requirements, or the documented scope of the project.
